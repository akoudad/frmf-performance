# AI Assistant Evaluation Questions

## Supported
1. Who is the top scorer?
2. Who has the most red cards?
3. Who has the most yellow cards?
4. Which team has the most injury events?
5. Show the top 5 scorers.

## Filtered
6. Who scored the most goals for a selected team?
7. Which players received red cards in a selected round?
8. Show injury events for a selected team.

## Ambiguous
9. Who is the best player?
Expected: ask what "best" means.

## Unsupported
10. Who is the best passer?
Expected: explain that pass data is unavailable.

11. Who has the most assists?
Expected: explain that assist data is unavailable.

12. Which players are currently suspended?
Expected: explain that suspension data is unavailable.
